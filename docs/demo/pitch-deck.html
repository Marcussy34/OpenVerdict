02 / 04

The problem

Truth has no referee

  • 1
    High-stakes outcomes, one trusted decider. Markets, DAOs and platforms settle disputed questions through a whale vote, a private back office, or a single model.
  • 2
    The referees are stepping back. Professional fact-checking is being dismantled exactly as generative AI floods the feed.
  • 3
    Even "just ask five AIs" can't be checked. Votes and reasoning live in editable server logs; nobody can recompute how the answer was reached.
Example · where money settles

Polymarket & Kalshi

Billions in volume, trust at the last mile. Polymarket's disputed markets resolve by UMA token-holder votes; in March 2025 its "Ukraine mineral deal" market resolved YES with no signed deal after whale-weighted voting. Kalshi's outcomes are ruled by the exchange's own resolution desk.

Example · where information settles

Meta ends fact-checking

In 2025 Meta shut down its third-party fact-checking program in favor of crowd notes, where most proposed notes never display and the ones that do arrive hours late, while AI-generated content surges through every feed.

The solution

A decentralized verification protocol

  • 1
    A jury of rival AI models, all reasoning on Gonka. Five seats across three model families, drawn by Sui native randomness; every juror researches both sides of the live web, and the adapter refuses any inference host but gonkarouter.io.
  • 2
    Secret votes, public debate. Commit-reveal on Sui makes herding impossible, and 4-of-5 matching votes settle a claim in one round. When the jury splits, the revealed jurors defend their votes in a live courtroom-style debate, then a second round votes on the full record; deadlock ends honestly as UNRESOLVED.
  • 3
    Don't trust it. Recompute it. Sui is the judge: deadlines, thresholds and settlement are enforced in Move. The verdict is an on-chain certificate with a 0-100 Truth Score, every search, page, prompt and vote is hashed to Walrus, and your browser reruns 15 checks per run.

Bonded claims also resolve optimistically: an unchallenged proposal settles with zero AI spend, and a challenge convenes the jury with the loser's bond footing the bill. Funded and rewarded in SUI: requesters escrow claim budgets, validly revealed seats earn payout tickets, and anyone can back a seat with a Google account.
